## 🎯 Problem Statement & Solution

### Problem Statement
Industrial combustion of plastic, rubber, wood, and other waste materials releases harmful pollutants such as particulate matter, smoke, acidic gases, VOCs, and toxic odors into the atmosphere. These emissions pose serious risks to human health and the environment. Existing air purification systems are often expensive and difficult for small-scale industries to adopt. There is a need for an affordable, efficient, and multi-stage exhaust treatment system. This project aims to reduce harmful emissions by integrating multiple air purification technologies into a single low-cost solution.

### Our Solution
Our project proposes a **Toxic Gas Cleaner** to reduce harmful emissions from plastic, rubber, wood, and biomass combustion. The system uses a **Cyclone Dust Collector** to remove large dust particles and ash from the exhaust stream. An **Electrostatic Precipitator (ESP)** captures fine particulate matter using a high-voltage electric field. The exhaust then passes through an **Ultrasonic Chemical Mist Chamber**, where chemical mist neutralizes acidic gases and absorbs remaining pollutants. Finally, an **Activated Carbon Filter** removes volatile organic compounds (VOCs), toxic gases, and unpleasant odors. This integrated multi-stage approach provides an efficient, low-cost, and scalable solution for cleaner industrial exhaust emissions.

---

## ✨ Features

* **Multi-Barrier Filtration Pipeline:** 5-stage sequential purification targeting macro to molecular pollutants.
* **Clog-Resistant Pre-Filtration:** Cyclone separator removes heavy ash prior to fine filtration stages.
* **Active Fine Particle Removal:** Custom Electrostatic Precipitator (ESP) charges and traps sub-micron particulate matter.
* **Chemical Gas Neutralization:** Atomized mist chamber maximizes surface area for neutralizing acidic emissions.
* **VOC & Odor Scrubbing:** Activated carbon bed captures residual chemical vapors and unpleasant odors.
* **Low-Cost & Low-Power Design:** Driven by a standard 12V DC input bus, making it accessible for small-scale applications.

## 🛠 Complete Tech Stack

* **Hardware & Electronics:** High-Voltage Generator (12V DC input), 3× Ultrasonic Mist Modules, High-RPM Centrifugal Blower Fan.
* **Structural & Prototyping Materials:** Cyclonic chamber housing, metallic inner mesh, aluminum foil collection sheath, activated carbon porous matrix.
* **Chemical Solutions:** Dilute alkaline neutralizing solution ($NaOH$ or $NaHCO_3$ active solution).
* **Documentation & Modeling:** Markdown, ASCII Diagrams, Fritzing/Schematic tools.
